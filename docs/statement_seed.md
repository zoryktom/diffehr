# PhD Statement Seed

After leaving my prior master's program, I focused on building an independent
research artifact at the intersection of biomedical informatics and AI
evaluation. I created DiffEHR, an open-source framework for counterfactual
contract testing of clinical AI systems on paired synthetic EHRs. Rather than
asking only whether a model answers a static clinical case correctly, DiffEHR
tests whether the model changes its answer when clinically decisive evidence
changes, remains stable when irrelevant attributes change, avoids temporally
unavailable evidence, and cites the chart evidence behind its decision. This
work led me toward my central research interest: developing evaluation methods
that make clinical AI behavior auditable, reproducible, and safe enough for
translation into healthcare environments.
